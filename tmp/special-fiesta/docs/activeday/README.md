## Three things every role needs to know

1. **A participant's PIN and scan number are different.** The 4-digit **PIN** is a secret used only to sign in. The 4-digit **scan number** on the QR badge is public: a facilitator may ask for it when the QR code cannot be scanned. Never ask a participant to reveal their PIN at a station.
2. **The participant does not award their own points.** A facilitator scans the participant's QR badge (or enters the scan number), checks the name shown, and the app records the points.
3. **Corrections are role-specific.** A facilitator can undo a just-made station award or handout inside its short on-screen undo window. After that, a superadmin corrects the record with a reason; a court result that has been ended can likewise only be undone by a superadmin.

## Which guide covers the common jobs?

- **Participant:** sign up or sign back in, recover a forgotten nickname or PIN, show the badge, join or captain a team, follow match times and results, collect passport stamps, check points, chat, share photos and change an avatar.
- **Facilitator:** sign in, pick a station, open and close rounds, award fringe points by camera or scan number, handle duplicates and offline scans, run a court match, hand out swag, reset a participant's PIN and clear the sync queue.
- **Superuser:** prepare clusters, points, activities, swag, programme, facilitator accounts and courts; rehearse and start the tournament; run the live desk; place or correct matches; repair participants and teams; void or restore scans and swag claims; announce and moderate; monitor operations; produce final standings.

> Venue wording, colours and artwork can change with the event theme. The button labels, tabs and flows in these guides are the shared app flows.
