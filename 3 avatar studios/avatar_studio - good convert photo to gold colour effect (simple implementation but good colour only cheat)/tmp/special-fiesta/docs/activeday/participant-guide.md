## 1. Quick start

1. Open the Active Day link / QR provided by organisers.
2. Look for whether you already have an Activity account. On first use choose **New here — sign up**. If you signed up before, choose **I already have a nickname**.
3. Keep two different numbers separate:
   - Your **4-digit PIN** is private. Use it only to sign in.
   - Your **4-digit scan number** is public and visible under your QR badge; facilitators use it when the badge cannot be scanned.
4. Once signed in, the **QR** tab shows your badge. Show that badge at fringe stations and swag collection points.
5. Use the bottom navigation throughout the app: **QR**, **Teams**, **Board**, **Feed**, **Social**, and **Me**.

## 2. Sign up · **Join in**

Use this the first time you claim a nickname.

1. Select **Sign up**.
2. Choose your **Cluster**.
3. Enter a **Station name** using **3–20 characters**. Letters, numbers and underscore are accepted; spaces become underscores.
4. Choose a **Gender** (Male / Female) so the right avatar choices appear.
5. Choose and customise your avatar: outfit, hairstyle, accessories and backdrop.
6. Enter a **4-digit PIN**. Choose one you can remember for the whole event; do not reuse your station scanning number or another person's PIN.
7. Select **Sign up & join!**. Successful sign-up signs you in straight away.
8. Check the avatar, cluster and spelling on your profile. If the nickname is wrong, contact a facilitator or superadmin before collecting scans, because scans attach to this identity.

## 3. Sign back in · **Welcome back**

1. Select **Sign in**.
2. Enter your **Nickname** and the same **4-digit PIN** from sign-up.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. Correct nickname + correct PIN opens your badge. If either is wrong the app stays on the sign-in screen.
5. Use the on-screen keypad carefully; a PIN has four digits and no letters.

## 4. Find your nickname or reset a PIN

These are different problems. Use the recovery that matches what you've forgotten.

### Find your nickname (forgot your nickname, remember your PIN)

1. On **Sign in**, choose **Find my nickname**.
2. Choose your **Cluster**. The app shows that cluster's participants.
3. Type a few letters in the filter to narrow the list — leave it blank to see everyone.
4. Tap your nickname to return straight to **Sign in**, or select **Back to sign in** and type it.
5. Enter your PIN and **Sign in**.

> This public finder shows names inside a cluster for event identity lookup. It does not show PINs.

### Reset a PIN (remember your nickname, forgot your PIN)

1. Go to any facilitator.
2. Tell them your nickname; they locate your account and choose a new PIN.
3. Check they've found your name, not a similar one.
4. Sign in with the new PIN right away before your next station. Your old PIN stops working immediately.

## 5. Use your badge at a fringe station

Fringe stations award **fringe points** directly to you. The common day pattern is: **get scanned → check your points/passport → move to the next station**.

### Before you approach the table

1. Queue until the facilitator calls you forward.
2. Tell them which result you achieved (for example **First place**, **Second place**, **Completed**) so they can choose your tier.
3. Open **QR** and present the badge upright, screen unlocked and brightness up.

### Show the badge

1. Hold your badge steady. The facilitator's camera must see the QR code.
2. If required, read your **4-digit scan number** aloud. It appears under your badge and as **Scan number** on **Me**. Do not read your PIN.
3. The facilitator's scan records the award straight away; if they typed your number instead, they confirm your name with **Award points** first.
4. Watch the outcome with them — it tells you whether points were awarded, already collected, held, rejected or pending.
5. Move away from the table so the next person can step forward.

### After the scan

1. Tap **Me**.
2. In the **Points** panel, check your **Fringe**, **Tournament** and **Total** points, and the **Stamps collected** count underneath.
3. If a passport is running, the line under the points shows your passport progress; tap **See my passport** to jump to your stamp list without hunting through menus.
4. If a stamp or score is genuinely wrong, ask the facilitator immediately or ask a superadmin to find your participant record — do not sign up again with a second nickname.

### Safe scanning etiquette

- **Never give your PIN to a facilitator.** Only your scan number.
- If a badge does not scan, your number is the fallback.
- Wait for the facilitator to confirm each outcome before moving to the next station.
- If no camera permission is available on the facilitator's device, your scan number lets the flow continue.

## 6. Create or join a team

Teams play the captain's ball tournament. You must be on a team to play tournament matches.

### Team status before you start

1. Tap **Teams**.
2. If you are already in a squad, the page opens **MY TEAM** directly with your roster, role and match history.
3. If not, the Teams screen offers **Create a team** and **Join with a code**.

### Create a team (you become captain)

1. Tap **Teams**.
2. In **Create a team**, enter a team name (max 30 characters).
3. Select **Create & be captain**.
4. The **MY TEAM** screen opens with you listed as captain.
5. Share the **Join code** (six characters, shown like `ABC123`) with your teammates so they can join.

### Join with a code (fastest route)

1. Ask your captain for the six-character code.
2. Tap **Teams** → **Join with a code**.
3. Enter the code carefully (upper/lowercase does not matter).
4. Select **Join team**.
5. The app opens **MY TEAM** with the roster and your role.

### Browse and join a team

1. Tap **Teams** → **Browse teams**.
2. Use **Search** to find your team's name, or scroll the list.
3. Tap a team name to open its profile: captain, match record, roster, lock status.
4. If the team is open, select **Join**.
5. If it says **Locked**, ask the captain to unlock it or give you the code — browsing can no longer add you until it's unlocked.

### Read the MY TEAM screen

- **Roster** shows captaincy and match record (wins–draws–losses).
- **Join code** is how new players attach to your squad.
- **Match history** shows finished and live team games. Winning margins, court and time are visible.
- **Team chat** (at the bottom) messages only your squad.
- Team points in this view are **tournament points**; they're separate from fringe points.

### Captain tools

Captains can shape the team roster. These controls appear only for the signed-in captain.

1. **Add by nickname** — type a participant's nickname exactly and select **Add to team**.
2. **Lock / Unlock** — lock when the roster is final. Locked teams accept code-joiners only if the captain reopens them; open teams can be joined from Browse. Unlock to let code joins in again.
3. **Make captain** — from the **⋯** menu on a teammate's row, confirm **Hand it over**. Captain duties move instantly.
4. **Remove member** — from the **⋯** menu, **Remove** and confirm. That participant can join another team straightaway; the points they already earned stay with their cluster.

### Leave a team

1. On **MY TEAM**, select **Leave team** and confirm.
2. If you're the captain, the earliest-joined remaining teammate becomes captain automatically.
3. If you're the last member, leaving disbands the team and removes it from tournament.

## 7. Aim for the final and plan your next match

### Feed tab overview

Tap **Feed** in the bottom navigation. Its sub-tabs split the day's play:

- **Results** — match-by-match captain's ball results as they're played.
- **Line-ups** — who's playing where and when, including your upcoming games.
- **Agenda** — the whole day's running order.

### Results — what happened

1. Tap **Feed** → **Results**.
2. Each row shows the two teams, court, time, score and state.
3. **LIVE** means the game is on court now.
4. **ENDED** means final score; note a **0 – 0** without a tracked score means it wasn't recorded point-by-point.
5. **VOIDED** matches do not count toward points or standings.

### Line-ups — when do WE play?

1. Tap **Feed** → **Line-ups**.
2. Look for **YOUR TEAM** rows. Your team's upcoming game sits under the **YOUR TEAM PLAYS SOON** header.
3. Read the stage name, court and **~kick-off** estimate on the right.
4. Pull down to refresh. The list updates live.
5. If you're next on court it shows **Up next**; wait by the court it names.
6. New-match alerts pop over the screen — tap one to dismiss it.

### Agenda — where do I need to be?

1. Tap **Feed** → **Agenda**.
2. **NOW** marks what's happening this minute; **Up next** shows what's about to start.
3. Tap any item to read its location and notes.
4. **CANCELLED** items stay visible so you can see something was dropped — they do not award points.
5. Times are the organiser's programme, not match appointments from the tournament schedule. Use **Line-ups** for your court time.

## 8. Check points, passport and scan history

### My points (profile)

1. Tap **Me**.
2. The profile shows your avatar, cluster, station count and points summary.
3. Read the breakdown:
   - **Fringe** — points from station stamps.
   - **Tournament** — points from team results.
   - **Total** — fringe + tournament.
4. Under **Team**, check who you're rostered with, or choose **Find a team** if you're still unaffiliated.

### Board — cluster leaderboard

1. Tap **Board**.
2. **All clusters** are ranked by total. Your cluster is tagged **you**, and its bar shows share of the whole pot.
3. Each row breaks out **Fringe** and **Tournament** points plus registered player count.
4. **Top scorers** below names the highest individual scorers across the event, also with cluster.

### Scan history

1. Tap **Me** → **See my passport** — the route opens passport view. The same screen's **Scans** segment shows the full history list.
2. Tap **Scans** to see each entry: station, round and +points.
3. Entries crossed out were **voided** by a superadmin and no longer count.
4. Times on each row say when the stamp landed, not when you reached the station.

### Passport (collect stamps across stations)

1. Tap **Me** → **See my passport**, or open **Scans** and switch to the **Passport** sub-tab.
2. Each station appears once, with stamps for every **round** you've completed there.
3. Stamps stack — repeat a station across rounds and the pile grows; a **+N more** bubble means the list folds extras away rather than overwriting.
4. Stations not yet stamped show an empty **Not yet** slot.
5. The purple strip underneath tells you passport progress (for example `2 of 5 stations`) and any bonus state.

## 9. Chat and share photos

Use **Social** in the bottom navigation. It has two sections: **Chat** and **Photos**. When a small dot lights a section, something new has arrived.

### Chat

1. Tap **Social** → **Chat**.
2. Type in the **Say something…** box and **Send**. Press Enter to send; Shift+Enter makes a new line.
3. For history, **Earlier messages** loads more.
4. Avatars show face and cluster. **STAFF** badges identify organisers.
5. Threads move fast — keep messages short, friendly and event-related.

### Photos

1. Tap **Social** → **Photos**.
2. Tap **Share a photo** (it becomes **Share photo** once you're ready), choose an image from your phone.
3. Add an optional caption.
4. Select **Share**.
5. Choose **Newest** or **Most liked** to sort the wall.
6. Tap the heart on a photo to like it (solid ♥). Tap again to unlike.
7. Tap **Report** on anything that should not be displayed. Reports go straight to moderators.
8. You have a limited number of photo shares — the counter shows how many remain.

### From a team page

Team chats live inside **MY TEAM** — scroll to **Team chat** to message your captain and teammates. Unread dots signal new messages here as well.

## 10. Change your look

1. Tap **Me**.
2. Select **Change my look**.
3. Edit the avatar's appearance (character, skin, hair, outfit and backdrop) — face preview updates live.
4. Tap **Save** to keep the new look on the board, teams and chat. **Cancel** drops the change.
5. Close the editor to see the updated profile.

## 11. Sign out

1. Tap **Me**.
2. Select **Sign out**.
3. If the app says you are still signed in, check your connection and tap **Sign out** again.
4. Staff on shared devices should sign out at the end of their session.

## Participant fine print

- Titles on the profile page (for example **rookie** / **veteran**) are cosmetic.
- **Stamps collected** on your profile counts recorded scans — two rounds at one station count twice, so it is a scan count, not a distinct-station count. Voided scans stay visible, struck through, in scan history.
- Announcements pop over every screen and stay until tapped — they are the fastest route to schedule changes.
- If the app reports you are signed in after sign-out, the safest move is to retry on stable Wi-Fi rather than tapping through errors.
- Check **Feed → Line-ups** before heading to a court; court assignments can change minutes before a game.
