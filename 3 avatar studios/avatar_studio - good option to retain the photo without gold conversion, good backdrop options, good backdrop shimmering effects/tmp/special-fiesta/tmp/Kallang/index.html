How it was made

  • The boats' crews were painted without paddles. Each paddle you see is a separate cut-out, stitched to its paddler at a measured anchor point and swung through a rowing cycle — switch to the lab view and turn on anchor points to inspect the stitching.
  • Everything moves on named loops (dragon-boat stroke, kayak stroke, walk and cheer cycles, cloud drift) driven by a seeded clock, so the scene is identical frame-for-frame on every visit to the same moment.
  • 313 sprites were extracted from five magenta-backed sheets by the pipeline in tools/; they are served from /sprites/… and indexed in a generated manifest.
