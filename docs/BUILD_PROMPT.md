## 1. Mission

Build a complete, single-page, real-time WebGL experience in the existing React + Vite + TypeScript workspace. It must recreate the *feeling and motion grammar* of the 2013 film *The Great Gatsby* main-title sequence: symmetrical, multi-plane Art Deco frames made of beveled golden rails drawing outward around monumental chrome-and-gold typography, then parting as the camera flies through them.

Create an original, non-infringing design homage. Do **not** use film footage, poster art, the official logo, the proprietary Deco Pinstripe font file, the Tiffany & Co./Bazmark monogram asset, or any copyrighted image. Every frame, letter, texture, particle, and reflection must be generated procedurally in code.

Deliver a running production build, not a mockup, static image, video, or placeholder. Do not ask clarifying questions; implement the defaults in this prompt exactly.

### Three-order intent

1. **First order:** let a visitor watch a WebGL title animation with golden Art Deco frames around the title.
2. **Second order:** let that visitor control the sequence, inspect the frames in 3D, personalize the hero lockup, and read the research behind the design.
3. **Third order:** demonstrate researched visual fidelity, technical WebGL craft, and safe use of an existing cinematic visual language without licensed assets.

### Typography and frame language

- Build a custom condensed Art Deco alphabet. Do not load Atlas or another commercial Deco face.
- Alternate glyph treatment letter by letter: one glyph has **four concentric inline pinstripes with three carved channels**; the next is a **solid chiseled glyph**. Use this in the main `GATSBY` lockup and the supporting cast name cards when alternation is enabled.
- Extrude the type in 3D. The front face is mirror-polished platinum/silver with warm champagne-gold crest tinting; the side walls are 24k gold. Preserve visible bevel facets and dark hollow channels.
- Animate type with a symmetrical center-out reveal, a travelling diagonal specular glint, and an incandescent edge at the reveal frontier.
- Every golden frame rail is a 3D chamfered prism, not a flat 2D stroke. It has a flat crest, two 45° bevels, side walls, and mitered corners. Distribute rails across at least three Z planes so parallax and reflected spotlights reveal depth.
- Recurring vocabulary: cut-corner octagons, stepped skyscraper/ziggurat edges, 45° chevrons, diamond medallions, concentric parallel rails, 11/13/15-ray Chrysler-style sunburst fans, stepped keystones, and thin horizontal frieze rules.
- Scene atmosphere: recessed tessellated Art Deco fan-and-diamond wall, warm ballroom reflections, low fog, drifting gold-dust bokeh, and two slow premiere searchlight cones. No visible stock imagery.

### Render pipeline

- Three.js `WebGLRenderer`, antialiasing on, pixel ratio capped at 2.
- `ACESFilmicToneMapping`, exposure about 1.18.
- `FogExp2` in obsidian at density about 0.022.
- Procedural PMREM ballroom environment map built from an equirectangular canvas: dark warm ceiling, bright softbox/colonnade strips, cool sapphire accents, warm floor bounce.
- `EffectComposer` with `RenderPass`, `UnrealBloomPass` (strength ≈ 0.56, radius ≈ 0.64, threshold ≈ 0.78), a custom nitrate/grain ShaderPass, and `OutputPass`.
- Custom nitrate pass behavior: subtle chromatic displacement, animated fine grain, soft vignette, and an optional 1920s silver-nitrate mode with desaturation, projector flicker, and occasional vertical emulsion scratches.
- Do not use `OrbitControls` in directed mode. Enable it only in Orbit mode with damping, minimum distance 3.2, maximum distance 26.

## 4. The five-act sequence (exact stopwatch)

The master timeline is **38.0 seconds**, loops to zero, and advances with frame-rate-independent delta time capped at 0.05s. Each act owns exactly one 3D card rig at a fixed world Z position. Only the card whose time window contains the playhead is active, apart from deliberate fade transitions.

| Act | Label | Archetype | Start | End | Z | Top line | Center line | Bottom line |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| I | The Green Light & GG Gate | `monogram_gate` | 0.0 | 7.2 | 0 | `WARNER BROS. PICTURES & BAZMARK` | `GG` monogram | `NEW YORK · SUMMER 1922` |
| II | Leonardo DiCaprio | `ziggurat_chevron` | 7.2 | 14.0 | −26 | `STARRING` | `LEONARDO DICAPRIO` | `AS JAY GATSBY` |
| III | Maguire · Mulligan | `sunburst_octagon` | 14.0 | 20.8 | −52 | `TOBEY MAGUIRE` | `CAREY MULLIGAN` | `JOEL EDGERTON · ELIZABETH DEBICKI` |
| IV | Baz Luhrmann | `diamond_gateway` | 20.8 | 27.5 | −78 | `A FILM BY` | `BAZ LUHRMANN` | `PRODUCTION & COSTUME DESIGN BY CATHERINE MARTIN` |
| V | The Great Gatsby | `gatsby_hero` | 27.5 | 38.0 | −108 | `THE` keystone | `GREAT` frieze + `GATSBY` hero | `FROM THE NOVEL BY F. SCOTT FITZGERALD` |

### Per-act frame construction

1. **Act I — monogram gate:** interlocking architectural octagonal `GG` signet inside a nested cut-octagon gate; four-line vertical side columns; corner diamonds; stepped sunburst crown and lower finial; green dock light pulsing behind it. Start in silver-nitrate: the first ~0.35–2.55 seconds fade from monochrome projection flicker/scratch into full color and gold.
2. **Act II — ziggurat and chevrons:** three nested stepped skyscraper rectangles, double horizontal rules around the cast name, four pairs of flanking 45° chevrons, upper/lower diamond clasps.
3. **Act III — sunburst octagon:** nested cut-corner cartouche; 13-ray upper and 11-ray lower Chrysler fans; flanking diamond spears and long horizontal rays; inner rules around the billing.
4. **Act IV — stereoscopic gateway:** layered rhomboid, octagonal, and ziggurat portals at four Z planes; interlocking diamond lattices left and right; broad inner rules; searchlights catching the chamfers.
5. **Act V — hero lockup:** nested stepped plaque; dedicated upper stepped keystone arch around `THE`; wide-tracked `GREAT` frieze with three flanking winged rules per side; divider rule and diamond clasp above `GATSBY`; lower rule above the credit; multi-plane diamonds and chevrons; 15-ray crown and 11-ray inverted lower fan.

### Motion contract per card

Let `local = (currentTime − act.start) / (act.end − act.start)`.

- Frame reveal: smoothstep from `local −0.04` to `0.42`; golden rails sweep from their symmetry axes with per-segment stagger delay between 0 and 0.65; the leading tip glows molten gold.
- Type reveal: smoothstep from `local 0.14` to `0.50`; center-out wipe; opacity follows card opacity.
- Gate parting for Acts I–IV: smoothstep from `local 0.80` to `1.03`; left frame halves translate negative X and right halves positive X by up to 5.2 world units. Act V never parts.
- Card opacity: fade in from `local −0.15` to `0.04`; fade out from `0.94` to `1.08` (final act: `0.91` to `0.995`).
- Do not animate frame draw, text reveal, and gate parting independently of the shared timeline; scrubbing must show the exact corresponding state.

## 5. Camera and spatial choreography

### Directed Cinema mode (default)

- Begin each non-final act about `z = act.z + 12.2`, ease inward to `z = act.z + 9.0` during the first 78% of the act.
- During the final 22%, fly through the parting portal to the next act's start distance using a smoothstep; this is the signature Baz Luhrmann-style stereoscopic transition.
- Final act: start about `z = act.z + 15.2`, settle to about `z = act.z + 12.4` over the first 52%, then drift forward slowly for the remainder.
- Add an aspect correction: when viewport aspect is below 1.65, pull back by `clamp((1.65 − aspect) × 6.5, 0, 5.8)` world units. Narrow screens must never crop the sunburst crown or flanking wings.
- Add restrained idle breathing and pointer parallax (maximum ±0.56 X, ±0.36 Y world units). No dolly roll beyond a tiny pointer-driven Z tilt.
- Aim slightly below center so the full upper crown and lower credit remain visible.
- Active frame lighting must move with the active card: a warm key, warm rim, and cool back light. Bevelled gold must produce moving specular glints.

### 3D Orbit mode

- On entry, focus the active card (or jump to its hold segment and pause if the playhead is before 42% or after 85% of that act), target the card center, and set the camera about 9.2 units in front and 2.4/0.9 X/Y.
- Users can drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom. The camera's up vector stays world-up; no flipping.
- Timeline playback continues unless the act-focus rule above pauses it. Switching back to Cinema restores directed camera framing without resetting the timeline.
- The experience must remain understandable on touch: orbit hint visible in Orbit mode, no hover-only required action.

## 6. Functional objects and validation contracts

### `SequenceChapter`

- Fixed read-only object: `id`, `actNumber`, `label`, `archetype`, `startTime`, `endTime`, `zPosition`, optional top/middle/main/bottom lines, description.
- Invariants: exactly five entries; windows are contiguous, non-overlapping, and end at 38.0; `startTime < endTime`; unique IDs and Z positions.
- Relationship: one act → one 3D card rig. There is never more than one active act at a given playhead. Act V is the only card eligible for hero-configurator overrides.
- No empty state because acts are compiled data, not user data. If malformed, fail visibly at load with developer console detail; do not silently render zero acts.

### `HeroTitleConfig`

| Attribute | Type | Constraint / default | Validation behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| `keystone` | single-line uppercase text | default `THE`; trim; max 8 visible characters | Blank falls back to `THE`; overflow is blocked by maxlength, never truncated with a visible error |
| `preTitle` | single-line uppercase text | default `GREAT`; trim; max 18 | Blank allowed; renders no upper frieze line |
| `mainTitle` | single-line uppercase text | required for a meaningful lockup; default `GATSBY`; max 16 | Blank falls back to `GATSBY`; do not block submission |
| `subCredit` | single-line uppercase text | default `FROM THE NOVEL BY F. SCOTT FITZGERALD`; max 46 | Blank allowed; renders no lower credit |
| `pinstripeAlternation` | boolean | default true | When false, every glyph renders solid |

Rationale: this is a creative preview, so invalid/blank copy resolves to a safe canonical lockup rather than a blocking error. Inputs immediately uppercase; leading/trailing whitespace is ignored. There is no persistence: new sessions start at the default.

The frame around `GATSBY` must recalculate its width from the generated text width so custom titles never collide with or clip the plaque.

### `PlaybackState`

States: `playing`, `paused`, `ended-then-looping`. Transitions:

- Initial state is playing at 0.0.
- Play/pause button or Space toggles playing/paused.
- Reaching 38.0 in playing returns to 0.0 and continues.
- Scrub changes the playhead immediately but does not change playing/paused.
- Act jump sets the playhead to `act.start + 0.15`; it does not force playback.
- Replay sets time 0 and forces playing.
- Applying a custom hero title rebuilds only Act V, forces directed mode if necessary, jumps to Act V, and forces playback.
- Orbit entry may pause only under the focus rule in §5.
- Modals do not stop the underlying WebGL sequence; the visual continues as a live backdrop.

Forbidden transitions: do not jump to an act outside 0–4; do not show a negative time; do not allow the camera to remain controlled by Orbit while in Cinema mode; do not lose user hero text on a normal modal close without applying it.

### Other session state

- `cameraMode`: `cinema` | `orbit`; starts cinema.
- `nitrateMode`: boolean; starts false. If false, the automatic opening nitrate grade runs only during Act I's opening; if true, a strong silver-nitrate look applies throughout.
- `soundOn`: boolean; starts false. Sound starts only after a user gesture. If the browser blocks audio, keep visuals running and disable the sound control; do not throw an unhandled error.
- `hudHidden`: boolean; starts false. Hide all HUD panels and proscenium decoration, leaving one small eye control visible to restore. `H` toggles it.
- Active modal: `research` | `customizer` | none; both are overlay dialogs with backdrop and X close. No browser `alert`, `confirm`, or `prompt`.
- No persistence of preferences across reloads.

### `ResearchSection`

Four read-only sections: creative lineage, 3D frame choreography, bespoke typography, and WebGL implementation. Each includes numeral, title, subtitle, credits, summary, and bullet points. The dossier must explicitly credit the researched collaborators: Baz Luhrmann, Catherine Martin, Bazmark, Like Minded Studio/Luca Ionescu, Deva Studios, Method Studios, Silvana Azzi Heras, Finn Spencer, and Tiffany & Co. as inspiration context—not as endorsers or asset providers. Include the non-affiliation note.

## 9. Non-functional requirements

- Target 60 fps at 1920×1080 on a mid-range laptop; never block the main thread with synchronous texture generation after first paint.
- Cap device pixel ratio at 2.
- Throttle React HUD state synchronization to roughly 20 Hz while the WebGL animation loop remains unthrottled.
- Reuse geometries/materials where practical; dispose all GPU resources on unmount/reconfiguration.
- Correctly handle container resize and viewport orientation changes.
- Keep production bundle self-contained except for Google Fonts; the provided Vite single-file build must succeed.

### Accessibility

- All icon-only buttons require `aria-label` and a title/tooltip where helpful.
- Maintain visible focus rings on interactive elements.
- Provide a static HTML fallback message if WebGL context creation fails: “This title sequence needs WebGL. Your browser could not start 3D graphics. Please enable hardware acceleration or try a recent version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.”

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: do not autoplay camera motion for those users; present the hero composition and let them press Play. (Mark this as the only acceptable deviation from the default autoplay requirement.)

- Handle rapid timeline scrubbing, repeated act jumps, repeated Orbit toggles, and repeated custom-title applications without duplicated cards, leaked listeners, or stale render loops.

## 11. Acceptance criteria (BDD)

1. **Autoplay and loop**  
   Given a fresh load with WebGL available, when the page opens, then Act I begins at 0.0 with gold rails drawing around the monogram, and the sequence advances through all five acts and loops at 38.0 without a blank frame or console error.
2. **Hero frame fidelity**  
   Given Act V is active, when it reaches its hold, then `THE`, `GREAT`, and `GATSBY` are visible inside a symmetrical multi-plane gold frame, `GATSBY` alternates inline and solid glyphs, and the full crown and lower credit are within the camera frame at 1920×1080 and 390×844.
3. **Scrub correctness**  
   Given the user drags the timeline to 22.0 seconds, then Act IV's frame and copy reflect exactly that playhead and the camera focuses Act IV without a reload.
4. **Act selection**  
   Given the user activates the Act III button, then the playhead moves to its start + 0.15s, the active button and badge update, and audio receives an act-change shimmer if sound is on.
5. **Orbit inspection**  
   Given Cinema mode, when the user activates `3D Orbit`, then direct camera control stops, OrbitControls allows drag/zoom around the active frame, and returning to Cinema restores directed framing without resetting time.
6. **Custom hero title**  
   Given the user enters `ROARING`, `TWENTIES`, enables pinstripe alternation, and selects Render in WebGL, then a rebuilt Act V displays the new text, the plaque resizes to avoid clipping, and playback begins in directed mode.
7. **Fallback input behavior**  
   Given the center-title field is blank, when the user renders, then `GATSBY` is used with no blocking error or silent disappearance.
8. **Nitrate mode**  
   Given `1920s Nitrate` is off, then only Act I's opening is automatically treated; when it is on, the silver-nitrate grade and grain persist across acts until toggled off.
9. **HUD visibility**  
   Given the HUD is visible, when the user presses `H` or the eye control, then header, footer, and proscenium hide, and the eye control remains available to restore them.
10. **Research dossier**  
    Given the user opens Research, then all four sections plus the three SVG diagrams and non-affiliation note are readable, closable by X/backdrop/Escape, and the WebGL scene has not crashed behind the modal.
11. **Accessibility and keyboard**  
    Given a keyboard-only user, then every button is tabbable with a visible focus indicator, Space controls playback, 1–5 jump acts, O toggles orbit, and text inputs are not hijacked by shortcuts.
12. **Build quality**  
    Given the finished repository, then `npx tsc --noEmit` passes, `npm run build` completes successfully, and the served `dist/index.html` renders without unhandled runtime errors.
13. **Robustness**  
    Given repeated resizes, modal opens, act jumps, orbit toggles, and custom-title renders, then no duplicate canvases, stale animation loops, or GPU resource warnings appear.
